Recruitment Pipeline

Our Selection Process

We follow a transparent, rigorous onboarding system to identify outstanding teaching and management talents.

01

Online Screen

Submit your resume and cover letter. Our HR team reviews credentials against vacant parameters within 5 business days.

02

Pedagogy Demo

Shortlisted educators deliver a 30-minute demo classroom lecture or sit for a brief technical assessment session.

03

Panel Review

Meet department heads and student welfare deans to discuss academic framework strategies, counseling, and values.

04

Onboarding

Final reference checks, credentials audits, contract finalization, and enrollment into our educator induction program.
