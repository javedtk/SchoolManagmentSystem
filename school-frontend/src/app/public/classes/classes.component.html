Got Questions?

Academic Frequently Asked Questions

How are classroom sections assigned?
Sections are assigned based on enrollment capacity limits and diagnostic assessment performance to ensure optimal teacher-to-student ratios and individual attention.
Is there learning support for struggling scholars?
Yes! Daily peer-tutoring circles and dedicated teacher-led study sessions are hosted in the digital library catalog after regular class hours.
Are Advanced Placement (AP) credits transferable?
Aether Academy is fully certified by the College Board. AP course credits count toward national board exams and are recognized by elite international universities.
