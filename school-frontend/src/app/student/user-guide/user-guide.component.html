Aether Portal Student Guide

Welcome to your student dashboard. This reference details how to track attendance, grades, pay fees, and submit assignments.

calendar_today 1. Tracking Attendance

View your presence ratios under **My Attendance**:

  • The top card shows your overall attendance compliance rate.
  • Review the chronological listing of daily attendance registry logs (Present, Absent, Leave).

assignment 2. Homework Assignments Submissions

Submit homework to your course instructors:

  • Navigate to the **Assignments** tab.
  • If teachers have uploaded study materials, download them via the **Download** links.
  • Click **Submit Work** next to any uncompleted assignment to select and upload your homework file (PDF, ZIP, Image).
  • Once reviewed, your grading status and remarks feedback will update automatically.

emoji_events 3. Reviewing Grades & Results

Check exam marks cards under **My Grades**:

  • Grades are grouped by Exam terms.
  • Click **Download Report Card PDF** to generate and save a copy of your official transcript.

payments 4. Settling Outstanding Fees

Process tuition fees invoicing:

  • Under **Fees & Receipts**, review your outstanding bills.
  • Click **Pay Now** to proceed to the mock checkout portal. Once approved, transaction entries will move to **Payment Receipts** with options to download PDF receipt vouchers.
